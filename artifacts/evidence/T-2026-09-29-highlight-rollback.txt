[WHAT THE TWO TESTS HOLD]
aRefusedHighlightLeavesNothingProjected: the store answers refused(Notice.STORAGE); the tap must
reach the store exactly once; the storage notice must be reported; and the read that follows must
push marks for the answer that do not contain the projection - an absent entry or an empty list,
which is what "the store holds nothing there" reads as on the screen. A projection that outlived a
refusal would be a fake success, which is the trap the acceptance item was written about.
anAcceptedHighlightLandsThroughTheSameRead: the store now holds the written row, and the same read
must push it. This is the anti-tautology half - a read that always answered empty would satisfy the
refusal test while silently dropping every successful highlight.
